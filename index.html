About Us

Hi! We’re Jack and Caroline — a husband-and-wife photo and video team who love capturing life’s most meaningful moments. As a couple, we know how important it is to preserve memories that you can look back on forever, and we bring that same care and excitement to every wedding, engagement, and event we shoot.

Our focus is simple: authentic, timeless photography and videography at a price that won’t break the bank. We believe everyone deserves beautiful memories of their special day, and we work closely with you to create a relaxed, enjoyable experience from start to finish.

While weddings are our specialty, we also love photographing engagements, family portraits, and all kinds of life events. Whether you’re saying “I do,” celebrating milestones, or just want to capture a season of life, we’d be honored to be part of it.

Let’s create something together that you’ll treasure for years to come!
